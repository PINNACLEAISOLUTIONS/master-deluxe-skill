# Growth Marketing & Copywriting Psychology Reference

## 1. Customer Awareness Stages (Eugene Schwartz)

1. **Unaware**: The audience does not know they have a problem.
   - *Strategy*: Lead with captivating stories, startling statistics, or relatable symptoms. No product pitching.
2. **Problem Aware**: They feel the pain but don't know solutions exist.
   - *Strategy*: Agitate the cost of inaction and define the core problem with sharp precision.
3. **Solution Aware**: They know solutions exist but don't know your specific product.
   - *Strategy*: Differentiate your mechanism/category. Explain *why* previous approaches failed.
4. **Product Aware**: They know your product but haven't committed to buying.
   - *Strategy*: Showcase specific proof, case studies, risk reversal (guarantees), and handle objections.
5. **Most Aware**: Ready to buy; only need terms, price, or urgency.
   - *Strategy*: Direct offer, clear pricing, frictionless call to action.

## 2. Persuasion & Psychological Mechanisms

- **Loss Aversion (Kahneman & Tversky)**: Pain of losing is 2x more intense than pleasure of gaining. Frame inaction in terms of wasted budget, lost hours, or falling behind competitors.
- **Jobs to Be Done (JTBD)**: Customers do not buy products; they hire solutions to make progress in their lives. Focus entirely on the desired transformation.
- **Objection Preemption**: Address skepticism directly before the reader raises it. Embed proof (testimonials, data, demonstrations) at the exact moments of friction.
- **Autonomy-Preserving Prompts**: Avoid high-pressure sales jargon. Use low-friction invitations that respect customer choice (e.g. "Explore the dashboard", "See how it works").
