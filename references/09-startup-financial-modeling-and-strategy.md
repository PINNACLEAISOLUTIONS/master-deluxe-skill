# Startup Financial Modeling, Unit Economics & Business Strategy

## 1. Core Financial Formulas & SaaS Metrics

### Monthly & Annual Recurring Revenue
- **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**:
  `MRR = New MRR + Expansion MRR - Contraction MRR - Churn MRR`
- **ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue)**:
  `ARR = MRR × 12`

### Unit Economics
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**:
  `CAC = (Total Sales & Marketing Expenses in Period) / (Number of New Customers Acquired)`
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**:
  `LTV = (ARPU × Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate`
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**:
  Target `>= 3.0x`. Below 1.0x indicates an unsustainable acquisition model; above 5.0x indicates under-investment in growth.
- **CAC Payback Period**:
  `CAC Payback (Months) = CAC / (ARPU × Gross Margin %)`
  Target: `< 12 months` for SMB, `< 18 months` for Enterprise.

### Burn & Runway
- **Net Burn Rate**:
  `Net Burn = Cash Outflows - Cash Inflows`
- **Cash Runway (Months)**:
  `Runway = Total Cash Reserves / Monthly Net Burn`
  Target: Maintain at least 18 months of operational runway.

## 2. Kotler Strategic Market Audit Framework

### Macro Audit (PESTEL)
1. **Political**: Regulatory shifts, trade policies, cross-border compliance.
2. **Economic**: Interest rates, capital availability, consumer spending power.
3. **Social**: Cultural preferences, demographic changes, work patterns.
4. **Technological**: AI disruption, infrastructure advances, API ecosystems.
5. **Environmental**: Sustainability mandates, carbon footprint disclosure.
6. **Legal**: Data sovereignty (GDPR, CCPA), antitrust regulations.

### Competitive Strategy (Porter's Forces)
Evaluate threat of new entrants, buyer bargaining power, supplier leverage, threat of substitutes, and competitive rivalry to design unassailable competitive moats.
