# Deep Research, Epistemic Rigor & Truth Verification

## 1. The Epistemic Truth Hierarchy

When performing research, evaluating claims, or answering questions, categorize all information into this 4-tier evidentiary hierarchy:

| Tier | Source Category | Confidence Weight | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Primary Technical Artifacts | 95–100% | Source code, official RFCs, SEC 10-K/10-Q filings, peer-reviewed clinical trials, patent disclosures |
| **Tier 2** | Verified Institutional Statements | 80–90% | Official documentation, keynote press releases from official spokespersons, government agency databases |
| **Tier 3** | Secondary Industry Analysis | 50–70% | Tech journalism, industry benchmark reports, well-cited independent case studies |
| **Tier 4** | Anecdotal & Opinion Content | 10–30% | Reddit discussions, unverified blog posts, speculative social media commentary |

---

## 2. Research Protocol & Corroboration

1. **Triangulate Non-Obvious Claims**:
   - Never report a controversial fact or forward-looking roadmap date without verifying across at least two independent Tier 1 or Tier 2 sources.
2. **Date & Version Anchoring**:
   - Always state the timestamp and software version associated with findings (e.g. "As of SDK v4.2 in October 2026...").
3. **Transparent Uncertainty**:
   - If evidence is conflicting, incomplete, or ambiguous, explicitly state the competing perspectives rather than fabricating a false consensus.
4. **Citation Discipline**:
   - Provide direct URLs or exact document references for all factual claims, metrics, and quotes.
